New catalog

Four short steps. Each one explains what it's for. Your progress saves as you type.

1

Name the catalog

Required
Use the team pattern so anyone can find it later: Channel · Slot · Week · Day. Tap a suggestion to start from it, then fill in the week and day.
2

Choose the brand

Required
A brand groups catalogs in lists and reports, for example by sponsor or partner. It doesn't change which products show or their prices.
3

Pick a starting point

Required
Duplicate when the show sells products similar to an earlier catalog. You keep what works and change only what's new. Start blank for a new show or a new partner.
4

Choose where it airs

Optional now
Pick the program from the channel schedule. The Shop panel turns on when the program starts and off when it ends. Not sure yet? Tick “Decide later” and it stays a draft.
